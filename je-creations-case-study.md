# Case Study: J.E. Creations
### Brand Website + Restaurant Management System (POS & Online Ordering)

**Client:** J.E. Creations — Jade Turner, Founder
**Industry:** Private Chef & Luxury Catering / Hospitality
**Location:** Metro Atlanta, GA
**Live site:** [jecreationsatl.com](https://jecreationsatl.com)
**Services provided:** Website Design & Development, Restaurant Management System (RMS) Setup — POS & Online Ordering

---

## Overview

J.E. Creations is a curated dining and hospitality brand founded by Jade Turner — offering private chef experiences, intimate catering, and culture-driven hospitality across Metro Atlanta. As a brand-new business owner entering the food service industry, Jade came to BrandsShift with a clear vision but no way to bring it online: no website, no booking flow, and no system to manage orders and payments as the business grew.

BrandsShift partnered with Jade to build both the public-facing brand experience and the operational backbone behind it — a polished website paired with a Restaurant Management System to handle POS transactions and online ordering.

---

## The Challenge

- Jade had a strong creative vision — menu concepts, a brand aesthetic, a name — but no technical roadmap for turning it into a working business online.
- As a first-time business owner, she needed a partner who could translate ideas into decisions: layout, color scheme, page structure, and site architecture, built around her specific offerings (private chef bookings, catering packages, brunch menus, and future ventures like a seasoning line and food truck concept).
- Beyond the website, the business needed a way to actually take orders and payments — a POS and online ordering system that could support catering bookings today and scale toward food truck and product sales down the line.

---

## The Solution

### 1. Custom Website Design & Development

BrandsShift designed and built a full brand website from the ground up, working closely with Jade at every stage — from initial concept through final launch:

- **Brand-driven visual identity** — a warm, upscale gold-and-black palette reflecting the "Built on Flavor, Driven by Culture" brand line
- **Founder & story pages** introducing Jade and the J.E. Creations mission
- **Multi-tier catering menu system**, including curated experiences like The Gathering Board, Tuscan Table Experience, The Cantina Experience, and a dedicated Brunch Menu (Golden Hour Brunch, Southern Luxe Brunch, and more)
- **Date Night** experience page for private chef bookings
- **"Book a Table" booking flow** to convert visitors into confirmed bookings
- **Portfolio/roadmap section** previewing upcoming ventures — a seasoning line, apparel, and the "Loaded Lansky's" food truck concept — positioning J.E. Creations as a growing lifestyle brand, not just a single service
- Fully responsive, mobile-first build with integrated social and contact touchpoints

### 2. Restaurant Management System (RMS) — POS & Online Ordering

Alongside the website, BrandsShift set up a Restaurant Management System to handle the operational side of the business:

- **Point-of-sale (POS) setup** for processing in-person and event-based transactions
- **Online ordering integration**, giving customers a direct path from browsing to purchase
- **Centralized order and menu management**, so Jade can update offerings and track orders without manual back-and-forth
